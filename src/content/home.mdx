## Golden Stitch Awards Nominations

FABRIC would like to acknowledge the amazing work of FABRIC users to date. We are soliciting nominations for four categories as a way to highlight the different ways FABRIC experiments are impacting our communities research. Winners will be selected by evaluating innovative uses of FABRIC resources, experiment design, and execution.

Nominations must actively use FABRIC and "Best FABRIC Experiment" nominations must be packaged as an artifact in the artifact manager.

Nominate yourself or a colleague for the following awards:

- Best Published Paper
- Best FABRIC Matrix
- Best FABRIC Experiment
- Best Classroom Use of FABRIC

Award winners will be announced during KNIT14 (April 2027). Nominations close on <b>February 12, 2027</b>.
